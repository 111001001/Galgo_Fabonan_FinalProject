About us

Conceptualized in 2015 by a team of WTA architects, led by William Ti and Beverly Locsin, the Book Stop Project is a pop-up library originally launched at Ayala Triangle during Dia del Libro 2016 at Makati.

Our mission is to build a space for books, reading, and learning, in easily accessible mobile places spread across the cities. In modern society where digital writing is accessible everywhere online, The Book Stop Project rethinks the architecture of libraries, in order to adapt to the modern era.
